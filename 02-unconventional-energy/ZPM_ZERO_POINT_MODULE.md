# ZPM — Zero-Point Module

**Classification:** Speculative research hypothesis  
**Context status:** Broad concept recovered; original engineering specification not available.  
**Privacy:** This document records the research topic only. It does not identify the individual who proposed it or include personal circumstances.

## Scope

ZPM is the user's term **Zero-Point Module**, associated in prior discussion with a broader "Golden Generator" concept and the question of whether quantum-vacuum / zero-point phenomena could ever be coupled to a controllable, useful energy-conversion process.

The existence of quantum vacuum phenomena is established physics. That fact does not demonstrate that a practical device can extract continuous net usable power from the vacuum.

## Evidence boundary

A 2019 review by Garret Moddel and Olga Dmitriyeva evaluates proposed zero-point-energy extraction approaches. It reports that proposed methods had not been reliably demonstrated. It analyzes nonlinear processing of the zero-point field, mechanical extraction using Casimir cavities, and gas-flow approaches based on stochastic electrodynamics. The authors find major thermodynamic obstacles for some approaches and describe experimental results for another as inconclusive.

The Casimir effect and other vacuum-related phenomena must not be conflated with a demonstrated source of continuous net power.

## Research questions

1. What exact physical mechanism does the proposed ZPM rely on?
2. What is the complete system boundary, including startup, control, cooling, pumping, shielding, and measurement power?
3. What energy reservoir is proposed, and how does the mechanism avoid merely releasing finite stored energy?
4. What output is predicted, in what units, over what duration, and with what uncertainty?
5. What controls distinguish the proposed effect from thermal gradients, electromagnetic pickup, chemical energy, instrument drift, and stored charge?
6. What independent experiment could falsify the proposed mechanism?
7. Does the proposed cycle comply with conservation laws and thermodynamic constraints?

## Required evidence before any performance claim

- Full input/output energy accounting.
- Calibrated instruments and documented uncertainty.
- Blinded or independently monitored controls where practical.
- Repeated runs and independent replication.
- Explicit accounting for transient stored energy and all auxiliary inputs.
- Safe, lawful experimental procedures and independent review.

## Status

The concept is included for rigorous investigation, not as an assertion that zero-point energy is practically extractable. The original ZPM proposal must be recovered before its specific mechanism can be assessed.
