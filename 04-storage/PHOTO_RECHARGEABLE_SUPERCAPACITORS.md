# Photo-Rechargeable Supercapacitors and Photocapacitors

**Classification:** Published research / demonstrated device concepts  
**HiveNet relevance:** Candidate for local energy buffering and autonomous low-power nodes.

## Research directions

- Photoactive materials integrated with electrochemical storage.
- Light-responsive electrolytes.
- Photovoltaic-supercapacitor integration.
- Flexible and all-solid-state photo-rechargeable micro-supercapacitors.
- Charge retention, cycling, illumination dependence, and energy conversion efficiency.

## Evaluation requirements

For each device, record illumination spectrum and intensity, active area, electrode and electrolyte composition, charging time, voltage, stored energy, discharge power, coulombic and energy efficiency, self-discharge, cycle life, and whether a separate external bias or charging source is used.

A device that responds to light is not necessarily self-powered under all conditions. Distinguish direct photocharging from an integrated photovoltaic cell charging a storage element.

## HiveNet integration hypothesis

Potential uses include trickle charging, local energy buffering, and power smoothing for sensors or intermittently active nodes. Device output, lifetime, packaging, and environmental tolerance must be compared with conventional photovoltaic-plus-storage systems before adoption.
