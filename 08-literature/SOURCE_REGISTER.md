# Energy Research Source Register

Initial sources and literature leads. Entries should be expanded with full metadata, access dates, methods, limitations, and claim-level notes before formal publication.

## Zero-point energy and vacuum phenomena

1. Cole, D. C., and Puthoff, H. E. (1993). "Extracting energy and heat from the vacuum." *Physical Review E*, 48, 1562. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.48.1562  
   Scope: Thermodynamic analysis of proposals involving zero-point radiation and Casimir forces. The paper's theoretical analysis is not evidence of a working energy-extraction device.

2. Moddel, G., and Dmitriyeva, O. (2019). "Extraction of Zero-Point Energy from the Vacuum: Assessment of Stochastic Electrodynamics-Based Approach as Compared to Other Methods." *Atoms*, 7(2), 51. https://doi.org/10.3390/atoms7020051  
   Scope: Review and analysis of proposed extraction methods; reports no reliable demonstration and discusses thermodynamic constraints and inconclusive experimental results.

3. Dmitriyeva, O., and Moddel, G. (2012). "Test of Zero-point Energy Emission from Gases Flowing Through Casimir Cavities." *Physics Procedia*, 38, 8–17. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.phpro.2012.08.007  
   Scope: Experimental investigation of emissions from gas flow through nanoporous membranes intended to test a proposed Casimir-cavity mechanism. Interpret results in the context of later analysis and replication.

## Source evaluation checklist

For each source, capture:
- Full citation and persistent identifier.
- Peer-review and publication status.
- Experimental vs theoretical vs review paper.
- Materials, apparatus, and system boundary.
- Inputs, outputs, auxiliary power, and measurement method.
- Controls, uncertainty, replication, and limitations.
- Claims supported by the paper vs claims made by others about it.
