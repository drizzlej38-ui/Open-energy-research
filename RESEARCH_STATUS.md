## Provenance rules

- **Recovered context:** A concept or constraint present in prior project discussion.
- **External source:** A claim or result supported by a cited publication or authoritative technical source.
- **New interpretation:** A proposed connection or explanation added during repository organization.
- **Missing context:** Original design details are not available and must not be reconstructed by guesswork.

## Important constraints recovered

- The solar filament concept was considered unlikely to work well planetside; the user favored an orbital-first architecture.
- System complexity and maintenance must not exceed the value delivered by a reliable energy-generation and collection system.
- A large purpose-built supercapacitor was discussed as a possible buffer/storage stage feeding a laser or maser for directed energy transmission. This is conceptual; no validated design or efficiency figures are established.
- Thermoelectric recovery was discussed as a grounded way to recover some energy from solar-heated systems, subject to temperature differential, materials, and conversion losses.
- Modified thermopile design details are not currently available.

## No implied claims

The repository does not claim that zero-point energy extraction works, that any concept produces net energy, or that HiveNet currently implements energy harvesting or energy management.
